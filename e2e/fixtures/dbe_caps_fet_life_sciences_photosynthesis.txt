Department of Basic Education — Curriculum and Assessment Policy Statement: Life Sciences Grades 10–12.

Transformations to Sustain Life — Energy.

Photosynthesis: the intake of raw materials, trapping and storing of energy, formation of food in chloroplasts and its storage, and the release of oxygen. Mention only of light and dark phases; no biochemical details of light and dark phases are required.

Importance of photosynthesis: release of oxygen, uptake of carbon dioxide from atmosphere, and food production through trapping energy.

Effects of variable amounts of light, carbon dioxide and temperature on the rate of photosynthesis should be discussed briefly together with graphs.
